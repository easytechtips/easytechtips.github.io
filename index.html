What is Cricut Design Space?

Cricut Design Space is the free software used to design projects and send them to a Cricut cutting machine. You use it to pick images, add text, arrange shapes and start the cut.

Cricut.com/setup is the official page that guides you through installing Design Space and connecting your machine for the first time.

What you need before you start

  • A Cricut machine (Explore, Maker, Joy or similar) with its power cable and USB cable
  • A computer (Windows or Mac) or a phone or tablet (iOS or Android)
  • A stable internet connection
  • An email address to create your Cricut ID
  • Bluetooth turned on, if you plan to connect wirelessly

Tip: Check the latest system requirements for your device on the official Cricut help pages before installing.

How to download Cricut Design Space

Download on Windows or Mac

  1. Open the setup pageType cricut.com/setup in your browser address bar and press Enter.
  2. Select your machineChoose your Cricut model, or continue to the Design Space download section.
  3. Click DownloadThe page detects your system. Choose Windows or Mac and wait for the installer file to finish downloading.
  4. Open the installerFind the file in your Downloads folder and double-click it.
  5. Follow the promptsAccept the terms, allow the install, and wait until Design Space opens.

How to set up your Cricut machine at Cricut.com/setup

  1. Unbox and power onPlace the machine on a flat surface with space to open the lid and move the mat. Plug in the power cable and press the power button.
  2. Open Design SpaceLaunch the app you installed in the previous section.
  3. Sign in or create a Cricut IDEnter your email and password. New user? Choose the option to create an account and verify your email.
  4. Choose New Machine SetupFrom the menu, select the machine setup option and pick your Cricut model.
  5. Connect by Bluetooth or USBFor Bluetooth, open your device Bluetooth settings and pair with your machine name. For USB, plug the cable into both devices.
  6. Follow on-screen instructionsDesign Space will register your machine and may update its firmware. Keep the machine powered on during the update.
  7. Make your first test cutLoad a mat with the material, press the load button, then press the Cricut button when it blinks.

Design Space on iPhone, iPad and Android

  1. Open your app storeUse the App Store on iPhone and iPad, or Google Play on Android.
  2. Search for Cricut Design SpaceChoose the app from Cricut, Inc. and tap Install or Get.
  3. Allow permissionsAllow Bluetooth access when asked so the app can find your machine.
  4. Sign in and connectLog in, select New Machine Setup and pair your machine.

Common Cricut setup problems and fixes

Design Space will not install

Close other programs, restart your computer, re-download the installer and run it again. On Windows, right-click the file and choose Run as administrator.

Cricut will not connect by Bluetooth

Keep the machine within 10 to 15 feet, remove the old pairing from your device, turn Bluetooth off and on, and restart the machine. If it still fails, use a USB cable.

Cricut machine not detected by computer

Try a different USB port, use the cable that came in the box, and avoid USB hubs.

Cannot sign in to Design Space

Check your email and password, reset your password if needed, and confirm your internet connection is working.

Firmware update stuck

Keep the machine on and connected, wait a few minutes, then restart Design Space and retry the update.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cricut Design Space free to download?

Yes. Design Space is free to download and use with a Cricut machine. Some images, fonts and projects need a paid subscription or separate purchase.

What is Cricut.com/setup?

It is the official page where you download Design Space and follow the guided machine setup.

Can I use Design Space on a phone or tablet?

Yes. It is available for iOS and Android, and also for Windows and Mac.

Why is my Cricut not connecting by Bluetooth?

Keep the machine within 10 to 15 feet, turn Bluetooth on, remove old pairings, restart both devices and try again, or use a USB cable.

Do I need internet to use Design Space?

You need internet to sign in, download and update. Some saved projects can be used offline on supported devices.
